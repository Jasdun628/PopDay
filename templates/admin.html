How routine context is used

Include phrases are the positive phrases PopDay looks for, such as investor day or analyst day.

Routine context phrases are ordinary events, such as earnings calls or annual meetings. They do not automatically block an alert.

They help PopDay explain weaker matches and avoid treating a passing mention of an ordinary meeting as an Investor Day announcement.
